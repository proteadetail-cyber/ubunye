What is a SHEQ retainer?

A SHEQ retainer is an agreement for regular, ongoing SHEQ support instead of one-off projects. The scope, support frequency and focus areas are agreed up front and can change as your business does.

It suits businesses that want a dependable SHEQ partner for OHS compliance, ISO system maintenance, risk assessments, safety files and training, without adding a full-time internal role.

When does a business need ongoing SHEQ support?

A growing workforce

More people, contractors or sites means more risk to manage and more records to keep.

No full-time SHEQ person

Compliance work still has to happen, with or without a dedicated internal resource.

Systems that need upkeep

ISO management systems, registers and safety files need regular maintenance to stay useful.

Findings to work through

Audit or incident findings take sustained follow-up, not a single visit.

What a retainer can include.
