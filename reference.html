Ubunye means oneness. We work that way.

Safety works when everyone on site owns it, from the director to the newest hire. UBUNYE (Pty) Ltd builds that shared responsibility into your business.

We translate the OHS Act and its regulations into clear systems, honest audits and training people actually remember.

What we take off your plate

Legal compliance audits

A full review against the OHS Act, COIDA and municipal requirements, with a ranked list of gaps and fixes.

Risk assessments

Hazard identification and control plans (HIRA) written for your site, your tasks and your people.

Training

First aid, fire marshals, working at heights, SHE representatives and toolbox talks.

Contractor management

Pre-qualification, induction and file checks so outside crews never become your liability.

Incident investigation

Root cause reports, Section 24 reporting and corrective actions that prevent a repeat.

Ongoing SHEQ support

A dedicated consultant on retainer: legal appointments, inspections, registers and management reports, all kept current.
