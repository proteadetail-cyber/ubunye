About UBUNYE Safety & Compliance.

UBUNYE Safety & Compliance is a South African SHEQ, OHS and ISO consulting business that helps industrial and corporate organisations turn legal and management-system requirements into practical systems.

Professional support. Practical delivery.

UBUNYE Safety & Compliance brings SHEQ, OHS compliance, ISO management systems, risk management and safety training together under one team, so South African businesses can deal with safety and compliance in one place.

The focus is practical: reduce risk, improve readiness and make compliance easier to maintain. Every engagement starts with a clear scope and a quote.

Who we support

Industrial and corporate businesses.

UBUNYE Safety & Compliance supports SHEQ managers, operations teams, compliance directors and business owners in manufacturing, industrial and corporate environments, including growing businesses without a full internal SHEQ function.

Where we work

South Africa.
