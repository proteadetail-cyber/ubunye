What is Incident investigation?

An incident investigation looks at an accident, injury, near miss or dangerous occurrence to find the immediate and underlying causes, rather than only recording the event.

Certain incidents must be reported to the relevant authority under the Occupational Health and Safety Act and COIDA. We can help you work out what applies to your situation. We do not provide legal advice; for legal questions you should consult a legal professional.

Who needs Incident investigation?

Employers after an incident

Businesses that need to understand what happened and respond properly.

Managers wanting an independent view

Teams that want a fresh, structured look at the facts.

Businesses with repeat incidents

Operations that see the same kinds of events and need the root causes found.

What UBUNYE provides.

Fact-gathering and record review

Written findings
