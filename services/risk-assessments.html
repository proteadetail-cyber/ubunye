What is Risk assessments?

A risk assessment is a structured look at what could cause harm in your workplace, how likely it is, how serious it could be and what controls are needed. It is often recorded as a hazard identification and risk assessment (HIRA).

Risk assessments are the foundation of safety management. They feed into safe work procedures, training, safety files and management systems such as ISO 45001.

Who needs Risk assessments?

Any employer

Businesses with employees, contractors or visitors on site need to understand and control workplace hazards.

Businesses that are changing

Companies introducing new equipment, processes, sites or contractors.

Teams preparing documentation

Businesses building a safety file or working toward an ISO 45001 system.
