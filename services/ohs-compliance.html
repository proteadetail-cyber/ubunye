What is OHS compliance?

OHS compliance means meeting your legal duties to provide a safe and healthy working environment. In South Africa the framework is the Occupational Health and Safety Act 85 of 1993 and its regulations, alongside related legislation such as the Compensation for Occupational Injuries and Diseases Act (COIDA).

An OHS audit or compliance review is a structured, evidence-based check of your workplace against those requirements. It looks at documents, conditions and practice, and produces findings that management can act on.

Who needs OHS compliance?

Employers and business owners

Anyone responsible for the health and safety of employees, contractors and visitors.

Operations and compliance managers

Teams preparing for internal, client or insurer reviews who need to know where they stand.

Businesses that are changing

Companies that have grown, changed processes or taken on contractors and need their compliance position checked.
