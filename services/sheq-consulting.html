What is SHEQ consulting?

SHEQ stands for safety, health, environment and quality. SHEQ consulting is the work of reviewing how a business manages those areas, finding where requirements are not being met, and helping put clear systems, responsibilities and records in place.

A SHEQ consultant does not replace your management team. They bring technical knowledge, an outside view and a structured way of working, so legal duties and management-system requirements can be met without taking over day-to-day operations.

Who needs SHEQ consulting?

Business owners and directors

People who carry responsibility for compliance and need a clear picture of where the business stands.

SHEQ and operations managers

Teams that need extra capacity, a second opinion or a structured plan to work from.

Growing businesses

Companies that need stronger SHEQ systems without building a full internal SHEQ function.
