What is Safety training?

Safety training builds the knowledge and awareness employees, supervisors and managers need to work safely and meet their responsibilities. Effective training starts with what the work involves, including the hazards, the roles and the legal requirements, rather than a generic course list.

Training can be pitched at management level, at supervisor level or at the workforce, depending on what your risk assessments and compliance reviews show is needed.

Who needs Safety training?

Employers with changing workforces

Businesses onboarding new employees or contractors who need consistent safety awareness.

Supervisors and managers

People who carry safety responsibilities and need to understand them.

Businesses acting on findings

Teams responding to audit findings, incidents or new risks.
