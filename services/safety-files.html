What is Safety files?

A safety file, also called a health and safety file, is an organised collection of documents showing how health and safety is managed. It typically includes policies, appointments, risk assessments, safe work procedures, training and inspection records and incident records.

Clients and principal contractors often ask for one before work or site access begins, and some situations, such as certain work under the Construction Regulations, require one formally. What belongs in your file depends on your operation, your client and the regulations that apply, and we confirm this with you first.
