About UBUNYE

A South African SHEQ and compliance partner.

UBUNYE Safety & Compliance is a South African business providing SHEQ, OHS, ISO, risk management and safety training services. We bring these together so businesses can deal with safety and compliance in one place, from the first quote through to ongoing support.

In the Eastern Cape, Nolan provides training, consulting, ISO support and OHS compliance audits, along with wider SHEQ services. In Limpopo and Mpumalanga, Mick provides training courses only. Explore available training courses or read more about UBUNYE.
